A household recording tool

Living across two currencies means keeping track of both the amount and its currency. CaLog was made for the family to record income and expenses and review household spending. The project is publicly described, but it is not advertised as an open-signup service.

The budget link may lead to a login screen. If you do not have an account, inability to sign in is an access limitation rather than a problem that these instructions can resolve.

Preparing a useful record

If you have access, check the date, actual amount paid and payment currency before entering a record. For example, 20 CAD and 20 KRW on the same day should not be added as though they were the same currency. If a receipt differs from a card authorization, check the settled amount.

These are general tips for preparing household records, not a claim that the product offers automatic currency conversion, bank connections or tax filing. Before comparing totals in different currencies, establish the reference currency and exchange rate.

Access and questions

Use only your own account and records you are permitted to access, and do not share login credentials. For access or project questions, use the contact details on the creator’s website. Open registration and a public release date are not promised.
