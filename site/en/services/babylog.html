Looking back on a baby’s day

This project brings together feeding, pumping, diaper and growth records so the last event does not have to be remembered from memory alone. It helps preserve context for family members reviewing the day; it is not a medical decision service.

A missing entry does not mean care did not happen. Confirm with the caregiver rather than drawing conclusions from the log alone.

Accessing the web view

  1. In an app you are authorized to use, find the QR authorization instructions for the web view.
  2. Follow the app’s instructions to approve the connection.
  3. Review the permitted records in the read-only web view.

It is not a public service where a visitor can open personal records from the web address alone. If editing is unavailable on the web, check the read-only scope first. Do not share the QR screen or authorization information.

Reading the records

An entry time and the time care actually happened may differ, so clarify which time is being discussed. After viewing records on a shared device, close the session and review access. Do not include real names, birthdays or family records in public questions or bug reports.

For account or access questions, use the contact information on the creator’s website. This page does not promise public signup or an app release date.
