AB Care Map

A reference map for Alberta emergency departments, urgent care centres and published estimated wait times.

A single place to look

This personal project brings facility locations and published wait estimates into a map and list, rather than making you look up each separately. Use it to compare location, distance and available wait-time information when exploring Alberta facilities.

A facility appearing on the map does not mean it can provide the care you need at that moment. Confirm services and operating details through the facility’s official information before relying on them.

An estimate is not an appointment

Numbers on screen are published estimates, not appointment times or a promise that you will be seen within that interval. They may change while you travel, and care is not provided solely in arrival order. Read update status and facility information instead of choosing on the smallest number alone.

A missing value or an old update is not a zero-minute wait. Treat connection errors, snapshot labels and demo labels separately from current data. Service labels and filters do not guarantee availability.

Who is it for?

It is a reference tool for people who want to understand facility locations and the status of published information. It does not diagnose symptoms, triage patients, book appointments or provide emergency response.

It is not operated or endorsed by AHS. Call 911 for a life-threatening emergency; for non-emergency health advice in Alberta, call 811. See the official Health Link information for its scope.
