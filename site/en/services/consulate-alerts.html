Updates in one place

The channel helps you notice new posts without repeatedly opening several consulate bulletin boards. It checks general notices, consular outreach, overseas travel safety information, and Korean community news.

It is an alert channel, not a booking desk, document-submission service or personal consultation service. Joining does not book a consular outreach visit or submit an application.

What an alert includes

Alerts include the board, post title and date, selected excerpts and an original-source link. The short text is an excerpt from the source, not an AI-written explanation. It is not a substitute for the complete post and attachments.

The service does not automatically read text inside images or attached PDFs. If dates or booking steps appear only there, open the source to check them. A short alert does not mean that the source contains little information.

Timing and limitations

Boards are checked hourly, so alerts are not immediate breaking news. Website or Telegram connection problems can delay delivery. Later edits to a previously sent title or post body are not sent as a separate update.

Before booking or travelling, reopen the source rather than relying on a saved alert. This independent service does not interpret consular rules or determine eligibility for an application.
