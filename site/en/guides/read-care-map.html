How to read AB Care Map

What to check in the map and list, how to interpret estimates and when to return to official information.

Written and reviewed by MYDY · Updated October 29, 2026

On this page1. Start with location and data status2. The lowest number is not a recommendation3. When information is unavailable

1. Start with location and data status

First make sure you are looking at the right area. Even when looking for a nearby facility, read its name and location instead of deciding on distance alone. Matching a list entry with its map location can help distinguish facilities with similar names.

Check update status and any error, demo or snapshot labels before using the information. Data shown during a connection problem should not be assumed current. Confirm actual services and operating status with the facility’s official information.

2. The lowest number is not a recommendation

For a fictional example, if facility A shows 40 minutes and B shows 70 minutes, that does not mean A is more appropriate for everyone. The number is not a personal appointment time accounting for travel, required care and local conditions. Actual experience can differ as circumstances change.

Do not read a blank estimate as zero minutes or compare an old value with a current one as though they were equivalent. A service filter cannot replace confirmation of available care. The map does not recommend a particular hospital or decide patient priority.

3. When information is unavailable

If information is missing, check your connection and try reopening the page. If it remains unavailable or unclear, consult the official facility information directly. For display problems, report your device, browser and what happened; there is no need to publish health information or your precise personal location.

For a life-threatening emergency, call 911 rather than continuing to compare the map. For non-emergency health advice in Alberta, call 811. See the official Health Link guidance. This article explains the screen, not diagnosis or care selection.
