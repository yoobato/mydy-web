PRACTICAL GUIDE

Subscribe to and check consular updates

Join the Telegram channel, check source posts and attachments, and manage notifications.

Written and reviewed by MYDY · Updated October 29, 2026

On this page1. Check the channel address2. Open the source after reading the title3. Missing or excessive notifications

1. Check the channel address

Follow the subscription button on the service introduction page or open t.me/korea_vancouver. This is the address designated by the independent service, not an official consulate account.

Check the channel name and address in Telegram’s app or web interface, then join. You do not need to send a passport, application or child’s details to this service to join. Telegram manages its own accounts and app settings.

2. Open the source after reading the title

  1. Check the board label and date.
  2. Use the excerpt to decide whether the notice is relevant.
  3. Open the original and read the full text and attachments.
  4. Apply or book through the official route named in that post.

Do not determine booking times, time zones, eligibility or required documents from an excerpt alone. Details inside images may be absent. Even if you saved an alert, reopen the source before acting because it may have changed.

3. Missing or excessive notifications

Hourly checking means a new post may not produce an immediate alert. If the channel has a post but your device does not notify you, check channel mute settings, Telegram notifications and operating-system permissions. If there is no channel post, you can check the official board directly.

Mute the channel to reduce interruptions, or leave it in Telegram to stop receiving channel updates. Menu labels vary by app version. Leaving does not cancel any consular application: the alert channel and consular services are separate.
